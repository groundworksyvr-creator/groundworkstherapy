His practice is built on assessment first. Informed by the Unified Health & Performance model, he looks at how the whole body loads, breathes, and moves before deciding where to put his hands — so treatment addresses the cause, not just the spot that hurts.

Pain is rarely a problem where you feel it. Find where it comes from, and the fix follows.

The approach

Find the cause. Build the foundation. Keep it there.

A lot of pain isn't a problem where you feel it — it's a problem in how the whole system loads, moves, and compensates. Every treatment runs on a simple loop: assess, intervene, reassess.

01

Assess

Range of motion, loading, and movement testing to find what's actually driving the pain — measured, not guessed.

02

Intervene

Hands-on manual therapy paired with exercise prescription, targeted at what the assessment revealed and built around your goals.

03

Reassess

Re-test to see what's working, measure the change, and adjust the plan so each session builds on the last.
