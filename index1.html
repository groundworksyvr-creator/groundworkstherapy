Services

What treatment looks like

Every session is built around your assessment. These are the most common reasons people book.

01

Therapeutic & Deep Tissue

Focused work for tension, stiffness, and chronic tight spots — neck, back, shoulders, hips. Pressure matched to what your body needs, not a one-size setting.

02

Injury & Recovery

Assessment-led treatment for strains, overuse, post-injury stiffness, and returning to activity after time off. Paired with home work to keep progress moving.

03

ICBC Active Recovery

Treatment for motor-vehicle injuries under an active ICBC claim, direct-billed. Whiplash, soft-tissue injury, and getting back to normal movement.

04

Maintenance & Movement

For active people who want to stay ahead of flare-ups — regular sessions built around how you train, work, and move day to day.

Meet Ben

Your recovery is worth investing in.

Ben Tsang is a registered massage therapist in the Edmonds area of Burnaby, working with people dealing with pain, injury, and the frustration of treatment that hasn't stuck — the recurring tight spot, the flare-up that keeps coming back, the injury that never quite resolved.

The Approach

Find the cause. Build the foundation.

Assess

Measured, not guessed. Range of motion, loading, and movement testing to find what's actually driving the pain.

Intervene

Hands-on manual therapy paired with exercise prescription, targeted at what the assessment revealed and built around your goals.

Reassess

Re-test to see what's working, measure the change, and adjust the plan so each session builds on the last.

Frequently asked questions

Yes, for active ICBC claims. Please email info@groundworkstherapy.ca before booking so we can confirm your claim details, then bring your claim number and accident date to your first appointment.
Direct billing is available for many extended health plans. Coverage varies by provider and plan, so it's worth confirming your massage therapy coverage with your insurer — and letting me know at booking so I can set it up.
No referral is needed to book a session. Some extended health plans ask for a physician's note to reimburse — check your specific plan if you plan to claim it.
We start with a short conversation about what's going on and your goals, then a movement and range-of-motion assessment to find what's driving it. From there we move into hands-on treatment, and you'll leave with a couple of simple things to do between visits.
Come as you are. You'll be draped with sheets throughout and only the area being treated is uncovered. If you'd like, wear or bring loose clothing (like shorts and a tank) for movement assessment.
Booking is online, any time, through the Book button. You'll be able to pick your service, length, and a time that works. If you can't find a suitable slot, get in touch and we'll sort something out.
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to change or cancel a booking, so the time can be offered to someone else. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged 100% of the treatment fee. Life happens — reach out if something comes up.
It depends on what we're working on. For an acute injury or an active ICBC claim, more frequent visits early on tend to help most; for maintenance, many people settle into a rhythm that fits their body and schedule. We'll figure out the right cadence together.
